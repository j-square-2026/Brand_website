About

I'm a writer, a solo traveler, and a thinker. I didn't go straight to a job after graduating. Instead, I packed one bag, sold the rest, and left. I've spent months on the road — moving through 20+ Indian states, sleeping in hostels and homestays, eating at dhabas, talking to strangers, and writing everything down.

I write about the road. About loneliness and solitude. About freedom and its costs. About ambition and its emptiness. About finding yourself when the world you were handed does not fit. My writing is honest — it doesn't pretend everything is fine, and it doesn't pretend the road is always beautiful.
